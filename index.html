PRIVACY POLICY

Member information deserves protection.

Effective September 6, 2026

AT A GLANCEMembers onlyNo sale of dataLimited access

1. Scope

This policy explains how IAFF Local 2938 (“Local 2938,” “we,” or “us”) handles information through the Local 2938 Member App. The app is intended only for authorized Local 2938 members and officers.

2. Information we handle

We use your name, email address, member number, account role, and membership status to create and verify access. Authorized administrators may also manage roster phone numbers and mailing addresses supplied to Local 2938. We store notification preferences and, when you enable push notifications, a device push token and basic device information needed for delivery. The app stores content that authorized officers publish, upload, or manage. Member chat is disabled. Ask 2938 searches approved Local documents; search requests are used to return results and are not retained as a question history.

3. How information is used

We use information to verify membership and control access, deliver app features, provide approved union resources, send requested or administrator-authorized notifications, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, administer records, and meet applicable legal or union obligations. Ask 2938 uses member questions to locate information in approved documents and is not legal advice.

4. Service providers and disclosure

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database services, private file storage, and server functions; Expo for app delivery and optional push-notification delivery; and Apple or Google for mobile distribution and notification delivery. These providers may process limited information on our behalf. Information may also be available to authorized Local 2938 officers when necessary to administer the app or union records, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect safety and rights. We do not sell personal information.

5. Security and retention

We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards designed to protect member information. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained while needed to provide the app and for legitimate administrative, union-record, security, or legal purposes. Some union records may need to be retained even after an account is closed.

6. Your choices

You may manage notification categories in Profile and device-notification permission in your device settings. You may request deletion of your app account from More > Privacy & data and cancel a pending request from the same screen. Requests may be limited when retention is required for legitimate union, legal, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

7. Children and policy updates

The app is intended for authorized Local 2938 members and is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the app changes. A revised effective date will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app may be sent to brennan@foundforgedco.com.
